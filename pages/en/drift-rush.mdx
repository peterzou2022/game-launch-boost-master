# Drift Rush

Drift Rush is a browser drift racing game built around controlled speed, sharp timing, and smooth corner exits. Instead of winning through raw acceleration alone, you need to balance your entry speed, hold clean slides, and chain better drifts to earn bigger rewards.

## How to play

- **W / Up Arrow** - Accelerate
- **S / Down Arrow** - Brake or reverse
- **A / D** or **Left / Right Arrow** - Steer
- **Space / F** - Handbrake drift
- **Shift** - Nitro or drift assist depending on the mode

## What makes it fun

- Long drifts feel smooth and rewarding.
- Tracks push you to improve timing instead of just speed.
- Different cars bring different handling styles.
- Unlocks and upgrades give each race a sense of progression.

## Helpful drift tips

- Enter corners under control rather than too fast.
- Use the handbrake with care so you do not spin out.
- Ease off the throttle during longer drifts to stay balanced.
- Start with shorter, cleaner slides before chasing huge combos.
- Try multiple cars to find one that fits your style.
