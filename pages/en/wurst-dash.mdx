# Wurst Dash

Wurst Dash is a chaotic online kitchen runner where a frantic sausage charges through blades, burners, saws, and crushers. Every run is about reading hazards, controlling speed at the right moment, and surviving one more ridiculous stretch of kitchen disaster.

## How to play

- Hold the mouse button or touch the screen to slow down.
- Release to speed forward again.
- Weave through saws, burners, knives, and crushers.
- Stay alive longer to push your score higher.

## Why this game stands out

- Funny cartoon accidents make every failure entertaining.
- Simple controls make it easy to learn in seconds.
- Fast reaction gameplay keeps runs tense and addictive.
- Great pick for players who like endless runners and score chasing.

## What to expect

Early runs ease you in with clear hazards, but the kitchen quickly becomes more crowded and demanding. Tight obstacle chains, faster timing windows, and nonstop pressure make every extra second feel earned.

## Survival tips

- Slow down before stacked traps instead of reacting late.
- Treat burners and saws like rhythm obstacles and learn their timing.
- Do not panic-release when narrow gaps appear.
- Focus on survival streaks first, then push for higher scores.
