# Arrow Puzzle

Arrow Puzzle is a relaxing online logic puzzle game built around sequencing, path reading, and clean decision-making. Study the board, find the arrows with a free route, and clear each piece in the right order until the maze opens up completely.

## How to play

- Look for an arrow that has a free direction.
- Tap arrows in the correct order.
- Clear space for the next moves.
- Finish the board with as few stalled moves as possible.

## Why people like it

- Relaxing visual style and steady pacing.
- Simple controls with satisfying logic.
- Great for short sessions on mobile or desktop.
- Easy to understand, but still rewarding to master.

## Tips for solving boards

- Start with arrows that obviously have open space.
- Avoid random tapping because one wrong move can block later options.
- Work from the edges when the board feels crowded.
- Reset your view and scan the whole board before forcing a move.
