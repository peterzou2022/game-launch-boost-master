ReactionTimeTest is a browser gaming website focused on fast-loading online games, simple access, and clear page layouts that help players start playing quickly.

We publish playable browser titles, game information pages, and supporting site content designed to make discovery easier across desktop and mobile devices. Our goal is to keep the experience direct: open a page, understand the game, and start playing without unnecessary friction.

## What We Do

- Publish browser-playable game pages
- Organize game content into simple, short-link URLs
- Support multi-language pages for broader accessibility
- Continuously improve design, usability, and page speed

## Our Focus

We care about:

- clean game presentation
- easy navigation
- consistent UI across the site
- useful supporting content for each game page
