## Why take a reaction time test?

### Improve gaming performance

Fast reactions matter in shooters, sports games, rhythm games, and competitive play. A reaction time test helps you see where you stand and whether your reflex training is working.

### Track reflex changes over time

Sleep, focus, stress, and practice all affect your results. Testing regularly gives you a simple way to measure progress.

### Build better everyday awareness

Reaction speed also matters outside games. It can influence sports performance, driving awareness, and how quickly you respond to visual cues.

## What is a good reaction time?

Reaction time is the delay between a visual signal and your click. For most adults, **200-250 ms** is common. **150-200 ms** is very strong, and many high-level players score even lower.

| Reaction time (ms) | Level |
| --- | --- |
| 150-200 | Very fast |
| 201-250 | Good |
| 251-300 | Average |
| 301-400 | Slower |
| 400+ | Retry after rest and better focus |

## Frequently asked questions

### What is reaction time?

Reaction time is the time between seeing a stimulus and making a response. This test measures that delay in milliseconds.

### What is the average human reaction time?

For visual stimuli, the average is usually around **200-250 ms**, though results vary by age, device, rest, and practice.

### Is 200 ms a good reaction time?

Yes. **200 ms** is a strong result and generally better than average.

### Can reaction time improve?

Yes. Practice, sleep, focus, and repetition can all help improve reaction speed.
